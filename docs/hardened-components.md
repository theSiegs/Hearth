# Hardened components

Change them in place, never rewrite them.

The parts below were reviewed, attacked with probe tests, fixed, and covered by regression tests (2026-11). Each one
handles edge cases that aren't obvious from a quick read. Change them with small edits that keep their tests passing.
Don't replace one with a "cleaner" version: if a redesign is truly needed, port every edge case and its test first,
and say so in the change. When another part gets the same review-probe-fix-test treatment, add it here.

| File | What it handles (tests in `ha_tests/`) |
|---|---|
| `counting.py` `merge` | A live "now playing" reading next to itemized readings counts as a total only, so one play isn't counted twice (`test_counting`) |
| `household.py` totals sources | Keeps the day's highest count across restarts. A drop of 5 min or less is accepted; a bigger drop counts as the source's new day before 6 AM and is ignored after. The count is capped at the time since midnight (`test_sources`) |
| `household.py` startup | A limit reached today stays held for 90 s after Home Assistant starts, but not on a reload. `group_packages` is cached once per recompute (`test_qc_fixes`) |
| `enforce.py` | A shared Switch keeps its real limit when it passes from one kid to another. Family Safety locks are applied again past midnight, and a pause locks platforms. The held set is updated as each platform's call succeeds. Hearth's own Family Link bonus isn't counted twice against a parent's. Extra time that has ended is ignored. A Family Link app pause is a parent's change only once it was seen intact (`pauses_intact`); one never shown is said and retried with a doubling wait (`pauses_not_taken`); apps of a pause call that failed part-way stay possibly held (`maybe_paused`) so letting go resumes them. A family pause locks a Family Link child's devices whole. `essential()` is the explicit system list (`SYSTEM_PARTS`, no `com.android.*` prefix: Chrome is held). A call that failed because the integration hadn't loaded yet is retried in 30 s (`_not_loaded_yet`). The schedule's Sleep locks a Family Link child's devices whole when Family Link has no bedtime of its own running (`sleep_locks_devices`); a device lock still wanted past midnight moves to the new weekday (`_ended_locks`, `_lock_devices`), and when one is named but not on 10 minutes into Sleep or after a lift ends (`SLEEP_GAP_GRACE`). Hearth's own put-back of a limit (Family Link's daily limit, a Switch's) is marked before the call (`lock_put_back_at`, `put_back_at`): cut off by a reload or failed after the write went out, the limit reading what Hearth wrote within 15 minutes is Hearth's, never a parent's grant; the hold is then no longer counted as on (locked again if still wanted). The quiet minute after a call is stored (`quiet`) and comes back after a reload. A resume that fails or is cut off part-way leaves none of its apps held for sure (`resume_unsure`, `maybe_paused`); the next pass keeps as held only those Family Link still has a pause record of (`_reconcile_resume`), and with no readable records pauses or resumes them all again. A swap from the device lock to pausing every app waits 10 minutes after the last swap (`SWAP_DWELL`) and after 4 swaps in an hour the device lock stays for an hour, said once (`_may_pause_every_app`; stored as `swaps`). Every app is paused only while the Always allowed apps are known (whole, or the stored last whole read: `fl_always_allowed`); otherwise the device lock. A hold's reason is taken before the call. Family Safety's platform lock and unlock are marked before the call too: a lock that timed out or was cut off after Microsoft applied it is possibly held (`fs_locking` in `_holding`, so letting go unlocks it) and a lock showing the end it asked for is Hearth's, never a parent's (`fs_derived_hold` `locking_until`); an unlock, the enforcement's or the midnight tick's (`async_lock_ends_tick`, which now keeps the quiet minute too), leaves `fs_unlocking`, and nothing locked within 15 minutes of it is Hearth's own unlock (`_fs_own_unlock`): the platform isn't held any more, locked again if still wanted, never "unlocked in the Family Safety app". A call refused before it went out (schema, no action) leaves no mark. `family_link_devices_held` says when a today-only daily limit must wait (`test_enforce`, `test_native_sync`, `test_seams_probes`, `test_bedtime_sync`, `test_enforce_own_writes`, `test_fs_upstream`, `test_platform_hardening`) |
| `native_sync.py` | A parent's Family Link bonus grant lasts while their share is left; extra time Hearth couldn't cancel stays known as Hearth's; Family Link extra time waits for a device in bedtime, School time or Hearth's own hold, and is given once it's free. Family Safety extra time whose write failed with anything but a schema's refusal (any error, a plain Exception too, per platform: the next platform still gets its own) is `possibly_given`: Hearth's while watched for 10 minutes (kept over a restart), adopted as Hearth's if Microsoft shows it ending when asked, dropped otherwise, never cancelled blind. Extra time in force ending at any of Hearth's ends of the day is Hearth's (`fs_bonus_ends`, not only the latest). A Family Safety request Hearth approved (`fs_request_approved`, from `asks._answer_family_safety`) is Hearth's own extra time on that platform (a device extra that lets the hold go while it lasts, no unlock over it); one whose call failed outright is possibly given, and the adults are told it may have gone through (`test_native_sync`, `test_fs_upstream`, `test_platform_hardening`) |
| `unlimited.py` | A total's minutes of a window reported after the settle are left out by each source's growth beyond the time since (ratchet, last window only). Unlimited started again while a window settles reopens it (`test_seams_probes`) |
| `pause.py` | Pauses are saved and survive a restart. Family Safety's downtime sensors are a bedtime by `downtime_start`/`downtime_end` (`test_qc_fixes`, `test_pause`) |
| `backstop.py` | A whole-device hold is remembered across a restart |
| `api.py` | Parent PIN guesses are checked one at a time past the free tries. Deeply nested JSON gets a 400 (`test_parent_pin`) |
| `api.py` `on_home_lan` | Whether a status post came straight from the home network (a phone's `local`): never through Home Assistant Cloud, never with a proxy's or tunnel's header, only from a private or link-local address at the connection's own other end (not loopback, an add-on's or a trusted proxy's), and only when the device asked for a home address, never the external URL. Anything unsure is not local. The endpoint's own `_local` gate is unchanged (`test_device_presence`, [contracts/device-presence.md](contracts/device-presence.md)) |
| `hub.py` `_profile_switch` | `hearth_tv_profile_changed` only between two posts of one run, within 15 minutes, in different profiles by the TV's own key: not from a restored status (restart, reload), a TV that came back, a ref learned later, a renamed profile, or a post naming no profile (`test_device_presence`) |
| `switch_approval.py` | Only an adult can answer, and the persistent notification never carries the request id (`test_switch_approval`) |
| `hub.py` | Tells a moved clock from a time zone (`utc_offset_minutes`). A day's counts never go down. A pairing ticket never takes over a device of another kind. One id is one device: `owned_by_another_kind` is asked by the pair view, `async_approve` and `async_redeem`, so no route pairs a TV or phone over a panel's id or a panel over theirs (`test_qc_fixes`, `test_tickets`) |
| `schedule.py` | Days off in a row, a day off ended early, and `off_now` for days that are over. `hearth.home_today` makes its own School time off (`Off.home`) next to any the day already has, `take_off` never reuses it, and `hearth.home_today_cancel` ends that one alone. A day kept home stays known one day after it (`_prune_home`) for yesterday's budget, shown nowhere (`homes_of`) and not cancelled. The day-off bonus on Family Safety's weekday limit and on a Switch is recorded (and saved) before the call (`temporary.begin`), and a held Switch gets it in what letting go puts back; on Family Link (a today-only daily limit) it waits while Hearth holds the devices and is written once they're let go, never folded into the weekday's kept limit (`_family_link_bonus`) (`test_schedule`, `test_places`, `test_home_today`, `test_platform_hardening`) |
| `weekly.py` | Half days when School is split by a break, and "until 00:00". A tonight-only wake earlier than usual starts the morning in the day's first awake mode, not the carried Sleep; `set_bedtime` / `set_nights` work out every day before changing any, and touch only the days given (`test_weekly`, `test_qc_fixes`, `test_invariants` E) |
| `bedtime_sync.py` | Writes only when what Hearth wants differs from what it last wrote (saved before the call); a reading equal to what was written is its own echo. A differing value is a parent's only once confirmed, past the quiet time, not Hearth's other writers' (a lift's moved start, extra time's moved end, its own today window of that weekday after midnight, every end extra time moved that day: `NativeSync.moved_windows`) and seen twice a minute apart; a value never shown didn't take (retried, told once), never a parent's. A failure's wait isn't reset by the old value still showing. "today" only with today's weekday; Family Safety rounded outward; no Family Link tonight write while a lift runs (`test_bedtime_sync`, `test_invariants` E) |
| `todos.py` | An undone reward stays undone. A kid's or their device's tick earns one item's reward at most 3 times a day (`too_often`; an adult still can), so a tick/untick loop can't flood the ledger, history and the adults' notices (`test_kid_todo_device`). Editing an every-N-days task keeps its start date (`test_chores`) |
| `places.py` | A Family Link move needs two location fixes. A reading too rough to place someone at home doesn't jump to a big zone (`test_places`) |
| `presence.py` + `presence_rules.py` | The kid's fix is set against an adult's track at its own time, not their latest position (a car together isn't apart). Unsure and unknown change nothing; nothing is confirmed while settling; a restored adult position keeps its stored age; no coordinate is stored, fired or logged. Messages when away opens on 5 minutes apart, goes back after 10 with an adult or at home, never flaps within 15, goes off after 6 hours unknown, and lifts time limits only; `Household.open_apps` keeps the device lock when a lock, pause, block, the schedule or School time holds the app (`test_presence`, `test_away_messages`, `test_invariants` D) |
| `fs_site_list.py` | Refuses a web page or a list that looks like a wrong download (`test_fs_site_list`). A site the list dropped that an adult also blocked by hand (`keep`, `hearth.block_site`) is never taken back, and is the hand block's from then on (`test_block_site`) |
| `app_blocks.py` | A block call that timed out is still Hearth's to lift. Launchers and keyboards are never blocked; Chrome (`com.android.chrome`) is blockable. A Blocked apps list that is missing or cut short is unknown, never "nothing is blocked": `blocked_packages` goes by the last whole list (`last_blocked`, stored) plus what the list shows, and `blocked_known` is False only when it was never read whole (`household.open_apps` then keeps the device lock) (`test_app_blocks`, `test_away_messages`) |
| `activity.py` own calls and trimming, `outside_apps.py` `_hearths` | A change is Hearth's own, never told to the adults as made outside it, when a call of the provider's names the app: every app of the call (`own_call`, not a record's first 50), from the moment the call starts (a resume of 150 apps is recorded two minutes later), also when it fails or is cancelled, kept over a restart; for `OWN_WINDOW` after it ended, counted back from the last whole look at the list, not from now. Records are capped per kind (`MAX_RECORDS` calls, `MAX_OTHER` of each other kind) so calls never evict the hold records the stray-pause release reads; a quiet read (the Nintendo applications list) is recorded only when it fails (`test_activity`, `test_outside_apps`) |
| `client_settings.py` | Hearth's own documents are exempt from the limits. Each device scope is capped at 256 KB (`test_qc_fixes`) |
| `glint_api.py` | Who may forget a phone. The start-screen options come from adults' phones only (`test_glint_agent`) |
| `glint_panel/hello.py` + `manager.py` hello (setup over the socket) | hello is open to any login and rules 1 to 3 approve at once on any but a kid's (the owner's choice; a kid's hello always waits); no room or an unknown room waits for an approval in HA. Rule 1 never takes a healthy panel over: a connected panel waits unless the login is trusted or the panel's own, an id that isn't the panel's `current_tablet` (an old tablet) waits unless trusted, and for `CONNECTED_GRACE` after attach every panel counts as connected (`seems_connected`: no rule 2, nothing in `offline_panels`). A refusal is checked before the at-once rules. A new secret stops the old one (its subscriptions get `unpaired`, then `unknown_panel`). An approved hello is sent again only to its own login (or an administrator's or adult's) until the first subscribe with its secret; its secret lives in memory only (a fresh one after a restart), and a hello for it on any other login stops it and waits with a card. A pruned waiting hello takes its card and notice; the notice is the administrators'. A TV's or phone's id is refused, a tablet id is the panel's own (`owns`), at most 20 wait, unseen 30 days go. A panel's proxy MAC is kept for life; a live one is adopted from its ESPHome entry only when no other panel holds it. New entity ids are `<device name>_<key>`, never the area twice (`test_glint_panel_hello`) |
| `entity.py` | Removes the entities of pools that no longer exist, only for keys that setup made (`test_qc_fixes`) |
| `media.py` | Rooms whose players have no name (`test_media`) |
| Settings (`panel.py` `hearth/config/set`, services) | Probed with 34 schema-valid odd values (built-in pool ids, missing entities, ghost people, cycles): none stops the household; keep new option consumers tolerant of missing targets |
| `hub.py` `_tell_failing_tv_checks` (with Hearth TV's `HaStatusReporter.java`) | A TV reports its accessibility service; 5 minutes off is told to the adults once, cleared when back (`test_qc_fixes`) |
| `household.py` `_lifts_document`, `_tomorrow_document`, `next_lock.until` | Lifts until midnight aren't listed; a category's minutes after its bonus leave the bonus out. Tomorrow's minutes are its day type's base (no bonus); apps only where they differ from today's. A next lock's end: a lock entity's own end (a morning time rolls to the next day), the schedule's next change, a lifted bedtime's own end (`test_tv_offline`, `test_client_contract`) |
| State document seam (TV and Home Assistant) | One fixture, `ha_tests/contract_fixtures/tv_state_document.json`, at six moments (evening with tomorrow, bedtime, bedtime lifted, a pause, YouTube allowed by a bonus, Video allowed until 6 PM): Home Assistant must still send what it says (`test_client_contract`) and the TV must read it as a family expects (`TvStateDocumentFixtureTest`). Its `document` is a whole state document with every top-level key [contracts/hearth-tv.md](contracts/hearth-tv.md) lists (`test_hearth_tv_contract`). Change both sides with the fixture: Hearth TV keeps a byte-for-byte copy in [theSiegs/HearthTV](https://github.com/theSiegs/HearthTV) (`android/app/src/test/resources/contract_fixtures/tv_state_document.json`) |
| `ha_tests/test_invariants.py` | Never-break rules over random cases: left values, whole minutes, counting, day scenarios. Keep it green; add a rule when you harden something |
| `family_link.py` `installed_apps`, `stray_pauses.py` | A Family Link app list cut short (`truncated`) reads as the last whole read plus what's shown, never "unknown": Hearth's pause of every app made the With time limits list outgrow the attribute limit, and the device lock and the app pauses swapped every few minutes. The last whole read is stored with what Hearth holds (`fl_installed` in enforce.py's store, loaded before the first run), so a restart while every app is paused doesn't swap either; with no whole read at all, a With time limits list cut short alone reads as what's shown plus the apps Hearth holds (`holding`), and any other list cut short stays unknown. `hearth.release_stray_pauses` touches only apps at a 1-minute limit that is on, that Hearth paused (action log), Family Link has no record of, that aren't blocked (a limit written back can lift a block) and that Hearth doesn't hold or may hold (`maybe_paused`), from whole With time limits and Blocked apps lists only, and not within 3 minutes of Hearth letting go of a Family Link hold (the list may still show a pause just resumed). Any error of one app's call (Family Link's are plain Exceptions) goes into `failed` and the rest go on; the answer comes back even when the sensor is gone by the end (`test_away_messages`, `test_stray_pauses`) |
| `bedtime_lift.py` | Family Link's bedtime tonight is moved (today only) and put back only while it reads Hearth's start or the one it found; a bedtime already under way is opened with bonus time on each device in bedtime, which is Hearth's own in native_sync's count and reset when the lift ends unless a parent's bonus came on top; a bonus or reset Family Link refuses is tried again, the start still moves; lifts, moves and bonuses survive a restart (`test_bedtime_lift`) |
| `fs_requests.py` | A request is new only when unseen and not resolved lately; it's resolved once missing from a fresh reading after a grace, as approved when extra time that isn't Hearth's own (any of its ends today) newly shows on its platform; an unavailable sensor, one without `requests` or a list cut short (`truncated`) resolves nothing, and a first reading cut short isn't learned from; known ids survive a restart; the fork's events and the sensor handle each request once (`test_fs_upstream`, `test_platform_hardening`) |
