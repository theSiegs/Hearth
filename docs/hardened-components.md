# Hardened components

Change them in place, never rewrite them.

The parts below were reviewed, attacked with probe tests, fixed, and covered by regression tests (2026-11). Each one
handles edge cases that aren't obvious from a quick read. Change them with small edits that keep their tests passing.
Don't replace one with a "cleaner" version: if a redesign is truly needed, port every edge case and its test first,
and say so in the change. When another part gets the same review-probe-fix-test treatment, add it here.

A Family Link app pause is a parent's change only once it was seen intact (`pauses_intact`); one never shown is said and retried with a doubling wait (`pauses_not_taken`); apps of a pause call that failed part-way stay possibly held (`maybe_paused`) so letting go resumes them. Every app is paused only while the Always allowed apps are known (whole, or the stored last whole read: `fl_always_allowed`); otherwise the device lock. A hold's reason is taken before the call. Family Safety's Xbox and Windows app pauses and resumes are marked before the call too (`fs_maybe_paused`, `fs_unsure_at`): apps of a call that failed with anything but a refusal (`refused_before_sent`), was cut off (CancelledError, re-raised) or that Family Safety's answer names as failed are possibly held (`_holding`, so letting go resumes them; app_blocks' `_fs_held` waits for them too); a failed resume leaves none held for sure; 3 minutes after, the next pass keeps as held only the apps Family Safety's own record still shows paused by it ("ours", `_fs_reconcile`, `fs_pause_records` by `app_id`), from whole lists only. An allow of Xbox apps while daily time alone locks the Xbox swaps the lock for a pause of every other listed Xbox app (`_xbox_swap`): never the allowed apps, a blocked one (a parent's, or Hearth's own: `fs_blocks`; an app blocked by Family Safety's own pause, "ours" or held, isn't blocked), the Xbox's own parts (`XBOX_NEVER_PAUSED`, `XBOX_NEVER_PAUSED_NAMES`) or a spared app; with Family Link's dwell and breaker (`_may_pause_every_app`, `_lock_kind` on the Family Safety key); the new hold first (`hold_first` for the Xbox too: apps paused before the unlock, the lock back before the resume).
