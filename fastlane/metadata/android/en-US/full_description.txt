Early development: expect rough edges and changes.

Hearth is an ad-free home screen for Google TV that keeps what makes Google TV work for a family: each person's profile, kids profiles, and Family Link's bedtime and screen time.

- Follows Google TV's profiles, kids' and adults': a home layout, wallpaper and Continue Watching per person.
- Works with Family Link: steps aside for bedtime and time's-up screens; puts itself on kids profiles and stays there; a parent PIN guards the settings.
- Profile Pairing picks your profile in Netflix, Disney+, Apple TV, HBO Max and Paramount+, and can type a saved PIN (optional).
- A calm home: your wallpaper, a Favorites dock, Continue Watching with cover art, your calendars and the weather.
- Home Assistant (optional): pop-ups with cameras and buttons, a dashboard drawn for the remote, and TV status for automations.
- Remap remote buttons; lock your profile; search where a title streams.
- No ads, analytics or account. Free software under the GPL v3.
