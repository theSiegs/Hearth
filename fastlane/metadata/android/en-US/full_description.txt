Early development: expect rough edges and changes.

Hearth is a home screen for Google TV without ads, tracking or recommendations. It shows only what you put there, and keeps working with the Google TV features families rely on.

- A wallpaper-first home with a Favorites dock, Continue Watching with cover art, and your app sections.
- Follows Google TV's profiles: each profile has its own layout, wallpaper and Continue Watching.
- Profile Pairing picks your profile in Netflix, Disney+, Apple TV, HBO Max and Paramount+.
- Works with Family Link screen time and bedtimes; puts itself on kids' profiles and stays there.
- Home Assistant: pop-up notifications with cameras and buttons, a dashboard panel, and TV status for automations.
- Remap remote buttons; lock your profile; search across streaming services.
- No analytics, ads or trackers. Free software under the GPL v3.
