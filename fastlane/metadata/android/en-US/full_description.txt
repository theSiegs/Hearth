Hearth is a quiet, ad-free interface for Google TV: your wallpaper, a dock of favorites, and what you were watching. No ads, no analytics, no trackers.

Made for families:
- Follows Google TV's own profiles: each person gets their own home screen and dock.
- Kids profiles: unapproved apps stay hidden, and Hearth's settings need the parent PIN.
- Profile Pairing: opens a number of apps (Disney+, Netflix and more) straight into the right profile.
- Sleep the TV after a stretch with no remote presses.

Home screen:
- A dock of favorite apps; Up for Continue Watching with cover art, Down for all apps.
- Left at the left edge opens Settings; Right at the right edge opens your Home Assistant dashboard.
- Accent colors, card styles, day/night and OLED-black wallpapers, custom banners.

Home Assistant:
- Notifications over any app, compatible with the "Notifications for Android TV / Fire TV" integration, plus live camera cards and action buttons.
- Slide-in dashboard panel, set up by scanning a QR code with your phone.
- Remote buttons that run scenes, scripts and toggles; TV status pushed to a webhook.

Also:
- Home Button Fix makes the Home button open Hearth on Google TV.
- Setup checklist that walks through every Android setting Hearth needs.
- Updates from inside Hearth; install and update HearthTube from Settings → Companion apps.
- Notifications panel, weather, TV input switching, daily settings backups.

Hearth is a fork of LTvLauncher by LeanBitLab, with ideas from Arc Launcher by Badis Meddouri, both built on FLauncher by etienn01 and osrosal. Licensed GPL-3.0.
